Welcome, Micro Explorer! The Knowledge Gates have been scattered across a mysterious microscopic world. Run, steer, jump, dodge moving hazards, collect glowing DNA coins and conquer 15 different obby zones to restore the Micro Core!

Third-person 3D-inspired obstacle adventure · Desktop + mobile

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

Desktop: W / ↑ run forward · S / ↓ run backward · A / ← move left · D / → move right · SPACE jump. Move diagonally to steer around obstacles.

Mobile: Use the directional pad to move and the JUMP button to leap.

This is an obstacle-course adventure: stay on the floating path, cross bridges, avoid moving hazards, weave between barriers, collect DNA coins and reach the glowing portal. Your character moves freely in all directions — not just jumping!

At each portal, answer a Biology question. Correct = +10 points. Incorrect = lose 1 life. You start with 5 lives. Feedback stays visible for at least 7 seconds.
